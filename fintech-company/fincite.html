About Fincite

Fincite's fincite • cios platform covers the wealth management process from digital client onboarding and WpHG profiling to advisory, portfolio optimisation, financial planning, 360° asset aggregation and reporting. It serves private banks, universal banks, Sparkassen, family offices, financial advisors and broker pools, and offers a 14-day free trial. Its partner ecosystem spans data providers, software partners, custodian banks, infrastructure providers and industry memberships.

What Fincite offers
