About Draivi

Draivi is a fintech-focused performance marketing company that uses proprietary technology and tailored funnels to deliver high-quality leads to lenders, fintechs and financial institutions. It operates in the Nordics, Germany, Spain, Mexico and South Africa, and reports about 1.5M monthly users and €29.7M turnover in 2025. It has been listed on the Financial Times FT1000 for three consecutive years and featured in case studies by Google and Meta.
