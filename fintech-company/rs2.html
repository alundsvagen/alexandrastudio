About RS2

RS2 provides an end-to-end, cloud-native omnichannel payment software and processing platform covering both issuing and acquiring for banks, financial institutions, ISOs, ISVs, PayFacs, fintechs and merchants. Founded in 1988, it reports 31bn transactions per year, serving 60+ countries with 7 offices across 5 continents and 400+ employees. RS2 p.l.c. is listed on the Malta Stock Exchange.
