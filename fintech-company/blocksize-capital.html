About Blocksize Capital

Blocksize aggregates crypto and tokenized stock (RWA) prices from more than 70 exchanges and 9,000+ pairs, delivering feeds over WebSocket/API at sub-250ms latency. It operates publishers, nodes and RPC endpoints across eight oracle networks including Chainlink, Pyth, Redstone, Supra, api3, Band, Tellor and Seda, and also acts as a validator on Solana and Polygon. It serves developers, startups, businesses, oracles and financial institutions, operating from compliant infrastructure in Germany.
