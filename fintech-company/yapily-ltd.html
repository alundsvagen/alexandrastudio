About Yapily Ltd.

Yapily offers open banking API infrastructure covering about 2,000 banks and institutions in 19 countries, with products for Pay by Bank, variable recurring payments, bulk payments, financial data access and data enrichment. It serves payment service providers, accounting and ERP platforms, banks and EMIs, and lenders, and positions itself as agent-ready, with strong business account coverage. Customers cited include Allica Bank, Pleo, Crezco, Yonder and Volume.

What Yapily Ltd. offers

  • Pay by Bank payments API
  • Variable Recurring Payments (VRP)
  • Commercial VRP
  • Bulk Payments
  • Financial data API
  • Data Plus transaction categorisation and enrichment
  • Validate account verification for onboarding
  • Yapily Connect (use of Yapily's PSD2 licence)

Who Yapily Ltd. serves

  • Payment service providers
  • Accounting & ERP platforms
  • Banks and EMIs
  • Lenders and credit providers
  • Developers and AI agent builders
