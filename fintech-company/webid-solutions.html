WebID Solutions

WebID Solutions provides online identity verification and digital signature services (KYC and KYB) for regulated companies.

About WebID Solutions

WebID offers a 'WebID Identity Hub' of GwG/EU-AMLR-compliant, eIDAS 2.0-certified identification methods, including agent-led video identification, automated ID checks, eID/NFC passport checks, an EUDI-Wallet connector, age verification, company (KYB) verification and qualified electronic signatures. It describes itself as a pioneer of video identification and says 300+ companies trust it, mainly in banking, fintech, crypto, telecom, e-commerce, e-health and e-government.

What WebID Solutions offers

  • VideoID live video identification
  • VisualID semi-automated identification
  • AutoID fully automated ID verification
  • AccountID bank-login identification
  • eID and ePassID electronic ID checks
  • EUDI-Wallet-Connector
  • TrueID reusable identity
  • AvsID age verification
  • CorporateID KYB verification
  • SignID qualified electronic signatures
  • WebID Portal no-code platform
  • ID Fraud Protect
