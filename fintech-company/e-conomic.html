About e-conomic

Visma e-conomic offers cloud accounting software covering automated bookkeeping and bank reconciliation, e-invoicing, expense management with company cards, budgeting, inventory and reporting. It serves Danish startups, SMEs, larger companies, and accountants and bookkeepers. The site says it is Denmark's most used online accounting program, behind 300,000 Danish businesses, with 300+ apps and integrations.

What e-conomic offers

  • Online accounting software
  • Invoicing and e-invoicing
  • Automated bookkeeping and bank reconciliation
  • Receipt/voucher handling
  • Supplier payments and debtor management
  • Expense management and company cards (Firmakort)
  • Budgets and forecasts
  • Inventory management
  • Payroll (Løn)
  • App marketplace with 300+ integrations
  • Courses, certification and support
