About Taktikal

Taktikal offers e-signature tools (Drop & Sign, Fill & Sign) and SmartFlows, a collaborative designer for step-by-step digital journeys covering onboarding, KYC/KYB and AML compliance. It serves financial services, insurance, legal and HR teams as well as government and non-profit organisations, and is ISO 27001 certified with GDPR and eIDAS compliance. It integrates with Salesforce and Slack and offers an API.

What Taktikal offers

  • Drop & Sign PDF e-signatures
  • Fill & Sign
  • SmartFlows digital journey designer
  • Simple (SES) and advanced (AdES) electronic signatures
  • KYC/KYB and AML identity verification
  • Automated registry lookups and reports
  • Customizable document templates
  • Salesforce and Slack integrations
  • API
