About Netcetera

G+D Netcetera (Netcetera AG) builds software for banks, payment providers and insurers, including EMV 3-D Secure services, tokenization, Click to Pay, digital banking platforms, digital wealth advisory, pension technology and digital identity solutions. Its 3DS services protect over 200 million cards across more than 1,000 European banks, and it is a central operator in the girocard system. It also offers a Digital Euro Integration Platform built on its Fusion Platform that connects PSPs to the digital euro ecosystem.

What Netcetera offers

  • EMV 3-D Secure (Directory Server, Issuer Service, 3DS SDK, 3DS Server)
  • Digital banking platform (online banking, mobile wallet, account information service)
  • Digital Euro Integration Platform
  • Digital identity (brIDge, CIAM, EUDI wallet, self-sovereign identity)
  • Digital wealth and financial advisory
  • Payment enablement and debit issuing processing
  • Payment fraud prevention and Verification of Payee
  • Pension technology
  • Click to Pay and Secure Remote Commerce
  • Tokenization and passkeys
  • Custom software, AI solutions and financial consulting
