About IDnow

IDnow offers a modular Trust Platform that verifies identities (document, biometric, eID, EUDI wallet), assesses risk (AML screening, age, address, IBAN checks), authenticates users and provides trust services such as SES, AES, QES, qualified seals and QEAA. It serves regulated industries including financial services, crypto, telecom, insurance, gaming, mobility and HR, and reports 145M+ transactions a year and 600+ customers. It also runs a partner program for resellers, managed service providers and technology platforms.

What IDnow offers

  • Document verification
  • Biometric verification and authentication
  • eID schemes and EUDI wallet support
  • AML screening
  • Age, address, driving licence and IBAN verification
  • Risk intelligence and fraud prevention (deepfake, synthetic identity detection)
  • Qualified electronic signatures and seals (SES, AES, QES, QSeals)
  • QEAA trust services
  • OTP and device authentication
  • Partner program for resellers and platforms
