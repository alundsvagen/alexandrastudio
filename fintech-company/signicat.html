About Signicat

Signicat offers a single integration point to more than 240 identity methods, including national eIDs, EU Digital Identity Wallets, ID document and biometric verification, and AML/KYC data sources. Its products cover onboarding (KYC/KYB), secure login, e-signatures (including Dokobit) and no-code trust orchestration for regulated businesses such as banks, insurers and payment companies. The site says over 21,000 companies use it, and it runs a partner program for ISVs, data providers and ecosystem partners.
