About Mastercard

Mastercard provides payment cards, consumer and commercial payments, money movement (Mastercard Move), open finance, cybersecurity and fraud prevention, and data insights for financial institutions, merchants, fintechs, SMEs, large enterprises and the public sector. It also runs partner programs such as Partner Advantage, Network Enablement Partners, the Mastercard Move Partner Program and the Start Path startup program, and is developing tools for AI-driven agentic commerce (Mastercard Agent Pay).
