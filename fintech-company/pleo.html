About Pleo

Pleo offers employee and vendor cards, expense management, reimbursements, accounts payable, multi-currency accounts and AI-powered spend controls that sync automatically with accounting software. It serves startups, mid-sized companies and enterprises, and says it is used by more than 40,000 companies in Europe. Cards are issued by Pleo Financial Services A/S under a Mastercard licence, and the company runs a technology partner programme with ERP, HRIS and travel integrations.
