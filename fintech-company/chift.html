About Chift

Chift offers Unified APIs, ready-made Agentic Syncs and 'Chift for Agents' (an MCP server, CLI, AI mapping and the Chifty assistant). Through one integration, software vendors, fintechs, banks and accounting firms reach 150+ financial software connectors across Europe. It focuses on European coverage, is GDPR compliant and ISO 27001 certified, and recently raised a €10.5M Series A led by BlackFin Capital Partners.

What Chift offers

  • Unified APIs for accounting, invoicing, CRM, POS, ecommerce, payment and PMS software
  • Agentic Syncs (ready-made bidirectional data flows)
  • Chift for Agents (MCP server, CLI, AI mapping, Chifty assistant)
  • Data layer for synced historical financial data
  • Integration monitoring dashboard
  • 150+ maintained connectors
