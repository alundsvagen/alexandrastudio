About Duna

Duna offers AI-native compliance infrastructure that automates business onboarding, case management, periodic reviews and ongoing monitoring for regulated businesses. Its platform includes no-code onboarding journeys with 20+ KYB modules, 210+ local registries, AI agents for document verification, and daily PEP/sanctions/media screening. Customers named on the site include Moss, bol, seQura and Plaid.

What Duna offers

  • AI-native KYB/KYC/AML compliance platform
  • Duna Onboard no-code business onboarding journeys
  • Automated case management
  • Lifecycle monitoring and periodic reviews (re-KYC)
  • Daily PEP, sanctions and adverse media screening
  • Policy management and legal agreement acceptance
  • Data orchestration across local registries

Who Duna serves

  • Fintechs and financial services companies
  • Marketplaces and e-commerce platforms
  • Enterprises with compliance and onboarding teams
  • Compliance analysts
