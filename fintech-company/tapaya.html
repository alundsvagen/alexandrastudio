About Tapaya

Tapaya offers a Tap to Pay / SoftPOS app and platform that lets merchants accept in-person contactless payments on Android devices they already own, with online onboarding, approval in about 48 hours and D+2 payouts. It serves merchants, e-shops and platforms or integrators across 36 European markets, offers a sales dashboard and developer APIs, and plans purpose-built hardware. The company is PCI certified, backed by Passion Capital, Depo Ventures and BADideas.fund, and has partnered with Nets Baltics on SoftPOS for the Baltics.

What Tapaya offers

  • Tap to Pay / SoftPOS payment app
  • Merchant payments dashboard
  • Platform and integrator payments APIs
  • Dedicated payment devices with Tap to Pay pre-installed (coming soon)
  • Blended and Interchange++ merchant pricing
