About Macrobanx

Advapay provides core banking and crypto-fiat wallet software (SaaS and on-premise), Banking-as-a-Service through a network of payment providers, and licensing help for PI/EMI, MiCA crypto, MSB, RPAA, Swiss SRO and AUSTRAC registrations. It was founded by banking practitioners and fintech entrepreneurs and serves neobanks, e-wallets, crypto-fiat wallets, e-commerce, crowdfunding and micro-finance businesses. The site cites 100+ clients, 5 offices and 50 people, and a technology partners marketplace of ready integrations for KYC/AML, BaaS and card issuing.
