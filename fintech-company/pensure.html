About Pensure

Pensure is a Copenhagen-based fintech that provides white-label digital tools for banks and pension providers, including Robo-Advice for needs assessment and onboarding, Holi for customer insights and upsell potential, and digital pension transfers under §41. Its solutions integrate with Pensionsinfo, BBR, Tinglysning and the CPR registry, and with clients' existing systems through an open REST API and webhooks. Customer cases include Nordnet and Honestus.

What Pensure offers

  • Robo-Advice for pre-meeting advisory and needs assessment
  • Digital onboarding compliant with IDD and good practice rules
  • Holi customer insights and upsell potential tool
  • Digital pension transfer (§41 process)
