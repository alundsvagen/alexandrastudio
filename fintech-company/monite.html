About Monite

Monite offers a single, customizable API covering accounts receivable, accounts payable, accounting integrations and payments, aimed at fintech SaaS, vertical SaaS, banks, neobanks and payment providers that serve SMBs. Platforms can go live in weeks and use the features to grow revenue and retention. Named customers include Mollie, Payoneer and Firstbase.

What Monite offers

  • AI Invoicing (accounts receivable) API
  • AI Bill Pay (accounts payable) API
  • Expense management
  • AI-OCR and data extraction
  • E-invoicing and regional compliance
  • Bill Pay Rails
  • Payment links
  • Accounting sync and reconciliation

Who Monite serves

  • Fintech SaaS platforms
  • Vertical SaaS platforms
  • Banks and neobanks
  • Payment providers
  • SMBs (end users via platforms)
