About Price Shape

PriceShape, a Danish company, offers e-commerce retailers, brands and wholesalers live market data covering competitor prices, stock levels and product performance, along with rules-based dynamic pricing automation. It also covers marketing budget optimisation, marketplace insight, distributor relations and cross-border data, plus an AI pricing analyst called Penny. The company is ISO 27001-certified, says it scans 1.3M+ webshops daily, and holds G2 Leader and High Performer badges.
