About Boost

Boost.ai offers an end-to-end conversational and agentic AI platform for customer self-service, internal support and agent assist across chat and voice channels, combining a hybrid AI approach with generative AI. It targets financial services, insurance, telecom and the public sector, and runs a partner programme with referral, resell/co-sell and OEM models. The site announces that boost.ai is joining Automation Anywhere.
