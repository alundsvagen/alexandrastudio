About Lune

Lune turns raw transaction data into actionable insights through its modular platform of Enrich (multi-lingual merchant enrichment with name, logo, category and carbon footprint), Target (hyper-personalized offers) and Engage (personal finance management interface/SDK). It serves retail banks, neo-banks, telco wallets and fintechs, citing 1.5bn+ transactions annually and 0.5 ms response time. Case studies include ADIB, D360, National Bank of Fujairah, Beyon Money and Alaan Pay, and it shows ISO 27001 certification.
