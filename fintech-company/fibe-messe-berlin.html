About FIBE (Messe Berlin)

Messe Berlin is Berlin's state-owned exhibition company, organising and hosting hundreds of events annually, including its flagship trade fairs Fruit Logistica, InnoTrans, ITB and Grüne Woche. It operates the Berlin ExpoCenter with 27 exhibition and event halls and about 190,000 square metres of event space, plus venues such as CityCube Berlin, Palais Berlin, hub27 and the Funkturm, which it rents out to external organisers.

What FIBE (Messe Berlin) offers

  • Organising international trade fairs
  • Venue rental for congresses, conventions and events
  • Berlin ExpoCenter exhibition grounds
  • CityCube Berlin, Palais Berlin, hub27 and Funkturm event locations
  • Event services for organisers (sales, marketing, conventions)
  • Ticketing for events
