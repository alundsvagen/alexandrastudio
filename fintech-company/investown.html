About Investown

Investown runs a ČNB-licensed real estate crowdfunding platform where retail investors fund developer loans secured by property, with fixed returns, no investor fees, a secondary marketplace and membership tiers. It also provides fast, flexible financing to real estate developers of 10 to 120 million CZK per year as an alternative to bank loans.

What Investown offers

  • Real estate-secured loan investing app
  • Developer project financing
  • Secondary marketplace for selling investments
  • Autoinvest
  • Premium membership with bonus yield
  • Affiliate and referral programs
