About Loxon Solutions

Loxon is a credit management software provider with more than 25 years of experience. It offers integrated lending, collection (SaaS and on-premises) and risk management solutions such as rating/scoring, collateral management, early warning and IFRS 9, plus an Oracle Financial Services (OFSAA) practice. It serves financial institutions in about 30 countries, mainly in the CEE, APAC and MEA regions, and was ranked #67 overall and #3 for customer satisfaction in the Chartis RiskTech100 2027.

What Loxon Solutions offers

  • Debt collection software (SaaS and on-premises)
  • Risk Suite: rating/scoring, collateral management, early warning, IFRS 9
  • Retail and corporate lending software
  • Oracle Financial Services (OFSAA) implementation practice
  • Basel II/III solutions
