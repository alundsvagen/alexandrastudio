About Kani Payments

Kani unifies fragmented payments data into one cloud platform for automated reconciliation, pre-formatted and bespoke reporting, analytics, Mastercard QMR and Visa GOC card scheme reports, and FCA CASS 15 safeguarding controls. It serves issuers, acquirers, BIN sponsors, processors, e-money institutions and digital banks, and is pre-integrated with 30 major payment processors. The platform is certified for ISO 27001 and SOC 2.
