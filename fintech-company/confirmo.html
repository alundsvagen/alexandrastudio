About Confirmo

Confirmo offers stablecoin infrastructure for businesses, including hosted checkout, recurring stablecoin subscriptions from wallets or exchange accounts, deposits, and bulk cross-border payouts via UI or API. It says it has 12+ years of operation and is licensed under MiCA as a Crypto-Asset Service Provider authorised by the Central Bank of Ireland. It also says it operates across all 50 U.S. states and the EU and serves sectors such as payroll, PSPs, iGaming, eCommerce, forex and prop trading.
