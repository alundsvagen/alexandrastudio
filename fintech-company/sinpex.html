About Sinpex

Sinpex offers an AI-driven platform that extracts evidence from primary sources such as corporate registries and shareholder lists across 200+ jurisdictions, automating business verification, UBO identification, AML risk checks and audit-ready reporting. It targets banks, PSPs and fintechs, claiming 70% faster processing, and clients receive a compliance whitepaper co-written with Deloitte. Customers shown include KfW, Riverty, lemon.markets and OTTO Payments.

What Sinpex offers

  • KYB / business verification software
  • KYC onboarding automation
  • UBO identification
  • AI-powered document data extraction from registries and shareholder lists
  • AML risk checks and ongoing compliance monitoring
  • Audit-ready compliance reporting
