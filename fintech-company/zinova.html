About Zinova

Zinova provides financial services to Swedish SMEs, combining digital tools with personal contact. It offers an analysis tool that connects to a company's bookkeeping system with a chat-based assistant, advisors available digitally and in its hubs, cash-flow-based loans, and mentors with hands-on business experience. It was founded by chairman Tommy Jacobson with the stated aim of building a new bank for entrepreneurs, and its analysis tool is set to launch in summer 2026.
