About Contovista

Contovista offers banks and financial service providers AI-based Personal and Business Finance Managers, multibanking, a transaction categorisation Enrichment Engine, client analytics, a Carbon Footprint Manager and a Smart Balance Optimiser for liquidity and interest optimisation. Its solutions integrate via API or on-premise/SaaS into digital banking platforms. Customers include Swiss cantonal banks, Valiant, Viseca, neon, VZ VermögensZentrum and Raiffeisen Austria, and the company now operates under finnova AG.
