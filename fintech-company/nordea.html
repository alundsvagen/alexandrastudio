About Nordea

Nordea is the leading financial services group in the Nordics, offering personal, business and corporate banking across Denmark, Finland, Norway and Sweden, along with asset management and investment funds. It is a publicly listed bank headquartered in Helsinki, and its community engagement focuses on financial skills and entrepreneurship.
