About Capital Market Partners

CMP is a specialised financial services advisory delivering hands-on consulting in capital markets, M&A, regulatory reporting, asset management systems, post-trade, regulatory implementation and private banking and wealth management. Its clients include Nordic banks, asset managers, central banks, insurance and pension companies, family offices, market infrastructure firms and energy and commodities trading houses. It has offices in Copenhagen, Aarhus and Oslo.
