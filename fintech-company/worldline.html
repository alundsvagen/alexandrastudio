About Worldline

Worldline provides in-store, online and omnichannel payment solutions, merchant acquiring, payment terminals and account-to-account payments for merchants. For banks and financial institutions it offers issuing, acquiring processing, open banking, ATM management, authentication, digital banking, digital currency and financial markets solutions. It is listed on Euronext (WLN) and runs local sites across many European countries and Asia.
