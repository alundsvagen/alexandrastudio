About Virtusa

Virtusa offers AI services spanning strategy, engineering, foundations, operations and experiences, alongside application build and modernization, data, cloud and platform work, operations redesign and enterprise transformation. It serves clients in banking and financial services, insurance, healthcare, life sciences, communications, retail, manufacturing and other industries. The site mentions a partner ecosystem and multiple offices but does not name them on the pages read.
