About SEB

SEB is a bank and insurance provider that helps individuals and businesses through advice and long-term relationships. Its site offers mortgages and mortgage transfers, personal loans, savings in funds and shares, pension savings, card services and Mobilt BankID. It serves both private and corporate customers and runs a branch network.
