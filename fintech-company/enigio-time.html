About Enigio Time

Enigio, a Stockholm-based company, offers trace:original, an open network for creating and sending legally valid digital originals such as electronic bills of lading, bills of exchange, guarantees, promissory notes, CMRs and certificates to any counterparty. It serves banks, corporates, logistics providers and record-keeping organisations. The site says the platform is aligned with MLETR, ICC DSI verified, approved by the International Group of P&I Clubs as an eBL system and ISO 27001 certified.

What Enigio Time offers

  • trace:original digital original documents platform
  • Electronic bills of lading (eBL)
  • Digital bills of exchange and promissory notes
  • Digital bank guarantees
  • Digital CMR transport documents
  • Digital certificates, mortgages and digital envelope
  • Interoperable, MLETR-aligned document transfer
