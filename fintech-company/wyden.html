About Wyden

Wyden offers banks and brokers a unified trading and operating layer covering the full digital asset trade lifecycle, from price discovery and smart order routing to treasury, settlement and accounting. It connects to more than 65 exchanges, brokers and OTC desks, and integrates with custody and core banking systems. Its solutions include retail and institutional brokerage and Crypto-as-a-Service orchestration. Headquartered in Zurich, Wyden is certified to ISO/IEC 27001:2022, SOC 2 Type 2 and ISAE 3000.

What Wyden offers

  • Institutional digital asset trading platform (Wyden Infinity)
  • Retail brokerage solution
  • Institutional brokerage solution
  • Digital asset treasury management
  • Crypto-as-a-Service orchestration and client-side solution
  • Order and execution management (OEMS) with smart order routing
  • Digital asset settlement, accounting and reporting
  • Connectivity to 65+ exchanges, brokers and OTC desks
