About Digital Finance Frankfurt

The site presents Corinna Egerer, who has a background in economic sciences and hosts and conceptualises business and political conferences in German and English. Her events include live, hybrid and streaming formats, with a focus on economics, science, politics and finance/FinTech. She is named as founder and CEO of Frankfurt Digital Finance, and her references include central banks, banks, ministries and industry associations.
