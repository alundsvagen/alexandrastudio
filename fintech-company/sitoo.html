About Sitoo

Sitoo offers a MACH-certified, cloud-native SaaS POS that runs natively on iOS and Android, plus a unified commerce platform that connects stores and online channels in real time. It is aimed at global enterprise retailers, especially in fashion, footwear, sports and outdoor, and covers store inventory management, self checkout and retail RFID. The site cites ISO 27001:2022 and SOC 2 Type II certification, AWS Retail Competency, and recognition in Gartner Market Guides.

What Sitoo offers
