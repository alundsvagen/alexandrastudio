About Envira

Envira combines satellite analytics, parcel-level data, financial modelling and regulatory expertise to help insurers, banks, agribusinesses and public agencies quantify and manage physical climate risk. Its products include Envira Agro for field-to-finance agricultural intelligence, Envira Flood for address-level flood risk, and Envira Advisory for climate risk consulting. The site says it won the EU Big Data from Space Award 2025 and Grunddataprisen 2026.

What Envira offers

  • Envira Agro field-to-finance agricultural intelligence platform
  • Envira Flood address-level flood risk analytics
  • Envira Advisory climate risk consulting and strategy
  • Climate risk metrics for credit, underwriting and portfolio stress-testing
  • Regulatory compliance support (CRR III, CRD VI, ESG)

Who Envira serves

  • Insurers
  • Banks and financial services
  • Mortgage credit institutions
  • Agribusinesses, growers, traders and food producers
  • Government agencies and public sector
