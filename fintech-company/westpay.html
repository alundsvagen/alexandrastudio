About Westpay

Westpay is a full-service fintech and payment provider that offers omnichannel payments (in-store, e-commerce and self service) with aggregated reporting through its Integrated Commerce platform. Its products include Android-based Carbon payment terminals, SoftPoS, the Swipe2Pay app that lets shoppers pick their payment method, Payment Application as a Service and a multi-acquirer solution with smart routing. It serves merchants, POS vendors and e-commerce platforms, and is listed on Nasdaq First North Growth Market.

What Westpay offers

  • Integrated Commerce (unified in-store and e-commerce transaction management)
  • E-commerce payment solutions
  • Payment terminals (Carbon, Android-based)
  • SoftPoS card acceptance on Android devices
  • Swipe2Pay payment application
  • Payment Application as a Service
  • Multi-acquirer solution with smart dynamic routing
  • POS and e-commerce platform integration
  • Acquiring assistance and local support
