About Boerse Stuttgart Digital

Part of Boerse Stuttgart Group, Boerse Stuttgart Digital offers a modular one-stop shop for crypto trading and custody, including OTC brokerage, a regulated multilateral trading facility for digital assets, and MiCAR-licensed, BaFin-supervised fiduciary custody with staking. It serves banks, brokers, asset managers and other institutions across Europe, and reports 200+ institutional clients and over EUR 5B in assets in custody.

What Boerse Stuttgart Digital offers

  • Crypto brokerage (OTC trading)
  • Regulated digital asset exchange (MTF)
  • Institutional crypto custody / custody-as-a-service
  • Staking solution with slashing insurance
  • Broker API and custody API
