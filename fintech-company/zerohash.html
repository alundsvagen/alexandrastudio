About zerohash

zerohash offers embeddable infrastructure for crypto trading and staking, real-time stablecoin payments, qualified custody and tokenized financial products, with regulatory coverage and compliance built in. It serves brokerages, banks, fintechs, payment providers, marketplaces and gaming platforms. Its case studies feature Public, Interactive Brokers, Alinea Invest and MoneyLion, and it has been recognised in the Deloitte Technology Fast 500 and CNBC's World's Top Fintech Companies.

What zerohash offers

  • Embeddable crypto buy/sell trading
  • Token staking
  • Qualified custody for crypto and tokenized assets
  • Stablecoin payments, payins and payouts
  • On & off ramps and account funding
  • Remittances
  • Tokenization engine and payment rails
  • Lending infrastructure
  • Onchain account linking (Auth)
  • Agentic finance suite
  • Regulatory and embedded compliance infrastructure
  • Liquidity and execution
