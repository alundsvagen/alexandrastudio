About Gini

Gini offers AI and OCR features that banks and insurers build into their mobile apps. These include photo transfer (Fotoüberweisung), QR-code payment, SEPA Request-to-Pay, payment initiation and mobile receipt capture. The site says its features reach millions of end users daily, and it shows the logos of major German and Austrian banks and insurers.

What Gini offers

  • Photo transfer (Fotoüberweisung)
  • Screenshot and PDF transfer
  • QR invoice scanner and QR payment
  • SEPA Request-to-Pay
  • Payment initiation
  • Gini Pay Connect
  • Cross-border photo transfer
  • Gini Smart / Smart OCR+ data extraction
  • Mobile receipt capture for insurers
  • In-app payment for insurance apps
