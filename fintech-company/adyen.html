About Adyen

Adyen offers enterprises a single platform and API for online and in-person payments, global payouts, risk management, authentication, card issuing and embedded finance. It serves retail, travel and hospitality, digital media, SaaS platforms and marketplaces, food and beverage, and financial services, and holds US, UK and EU banking licenses. The site cites €1.4T processed annually, 99.999% historical uptime, 150+ currencies, 200+ local payment methods and 29 global offices, and it runs a partner program with technology and service partner tracks.
