About Modular Finance

Modular Finance offers user-friendly software enriched with proprietary ownership data, aimed at making capital markets more data-driven. Its products cover IR platforms, regulatory news distribution, MAR insider compliance, IR websites and shareholder data for banks and finance firms, with data covering Nordic and UK listed companies and global portfolios. The site reports an NPS of 72 and 24/7 in-house customer support.

What Modular Finance offers

  • Monitor investor relations platform (share register analysis, investor targeting, CRM)
  • MFN regulatory news distribution platform
  • Strictlog MAR compliance platform for insider lists and PDMR
  • Datablocks live data modules for IR websites
  • Eklips investor websites on subscription
  • Holdings shareholder data platform
  • Dataflow shareholder data via API or MCP
  • Analyst consensus estimates, bondholder data and unlisted ownership data add-ons

Who Modular Finance serves

  • Listed companies' investor relations teams
  • Finance professionals
  • Banks and banking professionals
  • Advisors handling insider compliance
