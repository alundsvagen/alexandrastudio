About Dateio

Dateio describes itself as Europe's largest card-linked marketing and transaction enrichment ecosystem. It offers the Dateio Platform for cashback rewards that connects banks and retailers, and Tapix, an API that enriches transaction data with merchant information for app UX and analytics. Founded in 2013 by Ondrej Knot and Ivan Dovica, it says it works with 90+ banks and fintechs and 600+ European retailers, with 99.99% data accuracy.
