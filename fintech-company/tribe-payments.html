About Tribe Payments

Tribe Payments offers banks, fintechs, acquirers and payment businesses an API-led, cloud-based platform covering issuer and acquirer processing, white-label digital banking, payment gateway, POS and SoftPOS, 3D Secure, fraud and risk monitoring, Bank Connect and open banking. It says it is the only processor in Europe directly connected to all major card schemes. It also claims 99.99% uptime and compliance with GDPR, PSD2, ISO 27001 and PCI-DSS.
