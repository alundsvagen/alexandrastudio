About Movitz Payments

Movitz Payments offers a modular payment modernization suite for banks and fintechs, covering pre-payment and post-payment processes through a unified API. Its products are Universal Payee Verification (Swift Pre-Validation, J.P. Morgan's Kinexys, EU Verification of Payee), real-time payment tracking via Swift GPI, anomaly detection, market intelligence for cross-border payments in more than 170 countries, and self-service case management. Solutions can be hosted by Movitz or deployed on-premises, and the company states it is ISO 27001 certified.
