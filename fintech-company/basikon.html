About Basikon

Basikon offers a configurable, low-code, API-first platform that lets banks, lenders, lessors, brokers and merchants run the full financing lifecycle from origination and scoring through contracts, servicing, accounting and reporting, with native agentic AI modules. It covers eight verticals including asset finance, leasing, consumer finance, BNPL, factoring, microfinance, core banking and mortgage. It reports 40M+ contracts under management, 40 financial institutions in 21 countries, six years of profitability and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification audited by BSI Group.

What Basikon offers

  • Core-lending and core-leasing SaaS platform
  • AI governance and agentic AI modules for financial workflows
  • Origination, credit scoring and risk analysis
  • Contract generation and qualified e-signature
  • Servicing: direct debit, reconciliation, amendments, collections
  • General ledger and multi-GAAP accounting
  • Dashboards and regulatory reporting
  • Core banking (accounts, payments)
