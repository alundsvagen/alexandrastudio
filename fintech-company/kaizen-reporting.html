About Kaizen Reporting

Kaizen (Kaizen Regtech Group Limited) offers regulatory reporting assurance through its Kaizen Hub platform, combining forensic accuracy testing across 100% of reportable data, completeness controls, control frameworks and accredited training. It covers G20 regimes including MiFIR, EMIR, SFTR, CFTC, SEC, ASIC, MAS, HKMA, MMSR and SMMD, and its wider group also offers Single Rulebook, MAR360 and Shareholding Disclosure solutions. It has 15+ years of experience working with large financial institutions and their regulators.

What Kaizen Reporting offers

  • Kaizen Hub reporting assurance platform
  • Accuracy Testing (forensic testing)
  • Completeness Controls
