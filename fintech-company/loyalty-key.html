About Loyalty Key

Loyalty Key A/S is a Copenhagen-based provider of end-to-end loyalty ecosystems: card-linked cashback technology, APIs, branded apps, a hotel platform, a network of 2,000+ merchants, real-time analytics and in-house marketing. It powers programs such as Visa Cashback in Denmark, Danske Bank Cashback, SAS EuroBonus online shopping and formerly Spar Nord Cashback. The company is ISAE 3402 certified by BDO.

What Loyalty Key offers

  • Payment-linked (card-linked) cashback technology
  • Loyalty API integration
  • White-label loyalty app development
  • Hotel rewards platform
  • Merchant network of 2,000+ businesses
  • Real-time data and analytics dashboard
  • In-house loyalty marketing agency

Who Loyalty Key serves

  • Banks and financial institutions
  • Card networks
  • Large enterprises and airlines
  • Retailers, restaurants and webshops
  • Consumers/cardholders
