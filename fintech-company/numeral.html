About Numeral

The numeral.io site now redirects to Mambu's Payments page, which suggests Numeral's offering has become Mambu Payments. It lets banks and fintechs connect to payment schemes and partner banks, orchestrate payment flows, streamline Verification of Payee and run payment operations from one cloud-native platform. It can run standalone or alongside Mambu's core banking platform.

What Numeral offers

  • Payment connectivity to schemes and partner banks
  • Payment orchestration and workflow configuration
  • Verification of Payee
  • Payment operations management via a single API
  • Pre-built bank and scheme connectors
