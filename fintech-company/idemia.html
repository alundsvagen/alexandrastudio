About IDEMIA

IDEMIA operates through two businesses: IDEMIA Secure Transactions (payment cards, eSIM and IoT connectivity, HSMs and post-quantum cryptography) and IDEMIA Public Security (trusted access, justice and public safety, border control and travel, North American identity services). It serves governments, banks, telecom operators, airports and enterprises. The site cites 60+ years of experience, €1B invested in R&D over 5 years and 1,500+ active patent families.

What IDEMIA offers

  • Biometric identity verification
  • Cryptography and post-quantum security
  • Payment cards and card issuance services
  • Digital payment solutions
  • eSIM, IoT and connected car connectivity
  • Hardware Security Modules (IDEMIA Sphere HSM)
  • Access control and time and attendance
  • Justice and public safety biometric identification
  • Border control and travel solutions (BORDERGUARD, PaXpress)
  • Driver's license and DMV solutions
