About Tangany GmbH

Tangany offers a cloud-based, white-label Wallet-as-a-Service core custody solution secured with HSM and MPC technology, supporting over 250 cryptocurrencies, tokenized assets and NFTs. Add-on services include crypto accounting, KYC/customer compliance, staking, tokenization, stablecoin and smart contract custody. It holds a German Crypto Custody licence (BaFin licence 50085612) and says it is trusted by 60+ banks, brokers and financial institutions.
