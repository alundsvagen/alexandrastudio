About Open Payments

Open Payments Europe AB is a regulated payment institute under the supervision of the Swedish Financial Supervisory Authority. It offers a banking API platform for ERPs, accounting, invoicing and other business systems, covering payment initiation, account data, account verification, batch payments and cross-border payments. It emphasises PSD2 and GDPR compliance, integrated AML monitoring and custom branding.

What Open Payments offers

  • Unified open banking API
  • Payment initiation (PIS), single SCA and batch payments
  • Account information and financial data access
  • Account verification
  • Cross-border payments with real-time exchange rates
  • Supplier invoice payment flows
  • ISO file replacement and bank connection maintenance
  • AML monitoring

Who Open Payments serves

  • ERP and accounting software providers
  • Invoicing and business system platforms
  • Accounting firms and advisory practices
  • Businesses needing bank payments and cash-flow data
