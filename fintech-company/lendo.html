About Lendo

Lendo AB runs a free loan broker and comparison service in Sweden. Customers can compare personal loans up to 800,000 SEK, business loans and mortgages from up to 40 lenders, with only one UC credit check per application. It also offers a credit score overview, loan insurance and the Kreddy personal-finance app, has operated since 2007, and has sister sites in Norway and Denmark.
