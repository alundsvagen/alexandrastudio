The short answer

There is no single best fintech marketing agency; there is a best agency for the job in front of you. Choose by the job:

A new countryMarket entry servicesLocal network, regulation, first customers

Most fintechs selling to banks need two of these at once: something that builds trust at scale (brand, content, PR) and something that gets the buying committee into a room (events, partnerships).

How to choose a fintech marketing agency: eight questions

  • Have you sold to banks, payment companies or other licensed buyers? Six to ten people sign off a bank deal; ask how they reach each one.
  • Do you know the marketing rules for financial services? Consumer credit, investment and payments marketing each have rules. An agency should know where the lines are.
  • Who will actually do the work? Ask to meet them, not just the pitch team.
  • How many fintech clients do you have now, and are any competitors? Some agencies, ours included, take one client per vertical.
  • What do you report, and how often? Named accounts and meetings beat impressions.
  • What is counted and what is estimated? Pipeline estimates should be labelled as estimates.
  • What does your team need from ours? Hours a week, approvals, subject-matter experts.
  • How do we stop? Month to month, or a long lock-in?

Fintech PR and communications: what good looks like

In the Nordics, fintech PR works best when it is earned rather than pitched. A good fintech PR and communications agency, or an in-house team, should:

  • Lead with data. Journalists cover new numbers. Your own data (transaction trends, survey results, a ranking like our Fintech Awards) is the most reliable route into the trade press.
  • Get you on stages before it gets you in print. Panels at Stockholm Fintech Week, Nordic Fintech Week and the Nordic Fintech Summit put you in front of the same editors and buyers, and give them a reason to quote you.
  • Plan for bank approvals. A bank customer's name in a press release can take months to approve. Agree it in the contract.
  • Know the Nordic media. Breakit for Swedish tech and startups, Nordic Fintech Magazine and Sifted for fintech, plus podcasts and newsletters read by the industry.
  • Be ready for a bad day. An outage or a regulator's letter needs a plan written before it happens.

When you need a fintech event agency

You need an event agency when the deal depends on people meeting: a tier-1 bank that won't take a cold meeting, a market where nobody knows you yet, or a conference where a booth buys ten minutes with people who are also selling. A good fintech event agency designs the room around one question, brings a co-host the guests trust, fills the seats with the accounts you named, and reports who came.

Market entry services for fintechs entering the Nordics

Help for fintechs entering Sweden or the Nordics comes from four places:

  • Public trade and investment agencies: Business Sweden and Invest Stockholm help foreign companies set up and find partners.
  • Fintech hubs and associations: Stockholm Fintech, SweFinTech, Copenhagen Fintech, Goose Valley Ventures, and Fintech Farm in Helsinki, which runs the Nordic Fintech Summit.
  • The regulator: Finansinspektionen authorises payment and e-money institutions in Sweden; firms licensed elsewhere in the EU can passport in.
  • Go-to-market partners: people who already have rooms with your buyers. That is what our GTM service does, and our guide to selling to Nordic banks covers the rest.

Questions people ask

What is the best fintech marketing agency?

The best one for your job: brand and content agencies for positioning, performance agencies for paid demand, PR agencies for coverage, event agencies for time with named buyers, and market entry partners for a new country. Ask whether they have sold to banks and licensed buyers, who does the work, and what is counted versus estimated.

What does a fintech PR and communications agency do?

Earns coverage and reputation: data-led stories for the trade press, speaking slots, analyst briefings, customer announcements (with bank approvals planned in), and a plan for bad days.

What is a fintech event agency?

An agency that gets your target buyers into a room: executive breakfasts, partner dinners, panels, builder nights and build days, usually co-hosted with a brand the guests trust, with a report of who came.

What market entry services exist for the Nordics?

Public agencies such as Business Sweden and Invest Stockholm, fintech hubs and associations, the regulator Finansinspektionen for licensing, and go-to-market partners who already have rooms with your buyers.
