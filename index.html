How it works

Buying committees don't click ads. They show up.

Map the committee, not the lead

A fintech sale needs six to ten people at the buyer to agree. We name them, per target account, before anything is booked.

Find companies who already have your ICP

Not competitors — adjacent companies selling to the same buyer. They bring their room, you bring yours.

Co-host in person, in Stockholm

You're not a sponsor logo. You're on the invitation, in the room, and the registration list is shared.

Make the room visible

Content that tags and highlights who attended. The event reaches the forty who came; the posts reach the four thousand who didn't.

Measure, then re-run what worked

Every channel is tracked against the committee. What converts gets repeated in the second half of the term.

What does a growth hacker actually do for a fintech? +

In fintech the buying decision needs six to ten people to agree, and most of them can't be reached with advertising. So the work is: identify that committee, find adjacent companies who already have them as customers, co-host events those people will actually attend, and turn each room into content that reaches the people who couldn't come. It's closer to ecosystem building than to campaign management — the output is relationships and a list you own, not impressions.

Who actually does the work? +

I do with a bit of help. One client per vertical at a time, so you're not sharing me with a competitor. That's is wwhy there's a cap on how many engagements we run at once.

Why co-hosted events instead of paid channels? +

A fintech buying decision needs six to ten people to agree, and most of them never click an ad. Co-hosting borrows an existing audience that already trusts the partner, which is the fastest route to a room full of your ICP with no acquisition cost. The content layer then extends that room to everyone who couldn't attend.

What happens across the 8 weeks? +

Week one: ICP and buying-committee mapping, and access setup. Week two: partner shortlist and first outreach. Weeks three and four: first event runs and the content cadence starts. Weeks five to eight: second event, and calls booked off both guest lists. The count is checked together at week eight.
