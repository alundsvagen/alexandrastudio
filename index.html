What is B2B marketing, in practice?

What does it cost, all in?

45,000 SEK per month across an eight-week term. There is no ad spend on top, because there are no ads. Event costs are quoted separately and approved by you before anything is booked.

What counts as a qualified lead?

A named person at a company matching your ICP who has opted in — registered for an event, subscribed to your newsletter, or replied to outreach — and whose contact details you keep. Not a scraped list, and not an anonymous form fill.

What happens if you miss the target at week eight?

If 30 qualified leads and 5 held calls aren't there at week eight, the invoice for weeks five to eight is cancelled and you can end the engagement owing nothing further. You keep everything produced.

What does a growth hacker do for a fintech company?

In fintech the buying decision needs six to ten people to agree, and most of them cannot be reached with advertising. The work is to identify that buying committee, find adjacent companies who already sell to them, co-host in-person events those people will attend, and turn each room into content that reaches everyone who could not come. The output is relationships and a contact list you own, not impressions.

Do you work with competing fintechs?

No. One client per vertical at a time, which is why there is a cap on how many engagements run at once.
