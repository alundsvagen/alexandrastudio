What does working with Alexandra Studio actually look like?

What does it cost, all in?

45,000 SEK per month across an eight-week engagement. There is no ad spend on top because the model does not depend on paid advertising. Event costs are quoted separately and approved by you before anything is booked.

What counts as a qualified lead?

A named person at a company matching your ICP who has opted in — for example by registering for an event, subscribing to your newsletter or replying to outreach — and whose contact details you keep. It is not a scraped list or an anonymous form fill.

What happens if you miss the target at week eight?

If 30 qualified leads and 5 held calls aren't there at week eight, the invoice for weeks five to eight is cancelled and you can end the engagement owing nothing further. You keep everything we've produced.

Who is Alexandra?

Alexandra Grisanti runs Alexandra Studio from Stockholm, working exclusively with fintech companies. She also runs the Operator Network and publishes the Stockholm Growth & Fintech newsletter. Her work sits at the intersection of marketing, business development, community and the Nordic fintech ecosystem. Before consulting, she spent two years at Zwapgrid as a product designer and UX researcher.

What does B2B growth look like for a fintech company?

Fintech buying decisions often involve six to ten people across leadership, finance, product, engineering, compliance and procurement. Most of them will not be reached by a single ad. We identify the buying committee, find companies that already have relationships with them, create reasons for those people to meet, and turn those conversations into content that reaches the people who were not there. The output is relationships and a contact list you own — not impressions.

Do you work with competing fintechs?

No. We work with one client per vertical at a time. That protects the relationships, ideas and network we build with each client, and deliberately limits how many engagements we take on.
