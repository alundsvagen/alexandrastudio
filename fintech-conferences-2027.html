Which fintech marketing conference should you pick in 2027?

  • To meet Nordic banks: Stockholm Fintech Week, Nordic Fintech Week and the Nordic Fintech Summit. Smaller crowds and more bank people per square metre.
  • To meet the global payments industry: Money20/20 Europe.
  • To learn bank marketing craft: The Financial Brand Forum, if your buyers are US banks and credit unions.
  • For investors and the wider Stockholm tech scene: Techarena.

Whichever you pick, most of the business happens around the conference rather than on the floor: in the breakfasts, dinners and side events where twelve people can talk for two hours.

Money20/20 Europe side events

Money20/20 Europe runs 8–10 June 2027 at the RAI in Amsterdam. During the show, the evenings fill up with partner dinners, receptions and parties across the city. What works:

  • Invite early. Senior guests fill their evenings weeks before the show. Send personal invitations at least four weeks ahead.
  • Keep it small. A dinner for 12 to 20 named guests beats a party for 200 you have never met.
  • Co-host. Two or three companies that sell to the same buyer, without competing, share the cost and each other's networks.
  • Stay close to the RAI, or near the hotels guests already use, so nobody has to cross the city.

In June 2026 we ran a partner dinner in Amsterdam after Money20/20 Europe with Copenhagen Fintech, Swedish Fintech, Surfboard Payments, Movitz Payments, Narvi Payments and Griffin Bank. Our newsletter covered the show in Money 20/20: The Payments Jungle.

Stockholm Fintech Week side events

For 2027 the organisers have made side events official: 17 March is a full day of side events across Stockholm, the day before the conference on 18 March. That makes it the best day of the year to host a fintech breakfast or dinner in Stockholm, and the most crowded. To stand out:

  • Book the venue now. Good rooms for 15 to 40 people go first.
  • Pick a time slot others avoid. Breakfast on the side-event day, or a dinner after the conference, competes less than the evening of the 17th.
  • Invite the delegations. Copenhagen Fintech and other hubs bring member delegations to Stockholm Fintech Week and look for side events to join.
  • Give it one question. “Is growth a judgement call?” fills a breakfast; “networking” does not.

Nordic Fintech Week side events

Nordic Fintech Week is Copenhagen's fintech week, run by Copenhagen Fintech. The 2027 dates have not been announced yet; in 2026 the main conference days were 23–24 September at TAP1. Around the conference there are roundtables, receptions, a morning run and partner side events hosted by banks and fintechs. The same rules apply: a short guest list, a co-host, one question, and invitations out four weeks ahead. Our newsletter wrote about 10 fintechs at Nordic Fintech Week.

Is a Money20/20 booth worth it?

Sometimes. A booth buys presence: 10 to 20 minutes at a time with people who are often there to sell, too. Add up the cost first:

  • Floor space, priced by the organiser.
  • The stand itself. The show contractor's ready-made stand packages for Money20/20 have been listed from €4,800 for 9 m² and €7,500 for 18 m², excluding VAT and the space.
  • Passes for your team. Delegate passes have been listed from about €1,200 a person.
  • Travel, hotels in Amsterdam in June, and three days of your best people.

A booth is worth it when you need to be seen by a broad payments audience, or when your meetings are already booked and the booth is where they happen. It is rarely worth it when you sell to a short list of Nordic banks; a dinner beside the show buys more time with them for less. Questions people ask

When is Money20/20 Europe 2027?

8–10 June 2027 at the RAI in Amsterdam.

When is Stockholm Fintech Week 2027?

17–18 March 2027. 17 March is a day of side events across Stockholm; the conference is on 18 March at Münchenbryggeriet.

When is Nordic Fintech Week 2027?

The 2027 dates had not been announced on 10 November 2026. In 2026 the main conference days were 23–24 September at TAP1 in Copenhagen.

What is the best fintech marketing conference in 2027?

For Nordic banks: Stockholm Fintech Week (17–18 March), the Nordic Fintech Summit (13 May, Helsinki) and Nordic Fintech Week (Copenhagen). For global payments: Money20/20 Europe (8–10 June). For US bank marketing: The Financial Brand Forum (5–7 April, Las Vegas).

Is a Money20/20 booth worth it?

It can be if you need broad visibility with the global payments industry or already have meetings booked. If you sell to a short list of named banks, a side-event dinner for 12 to 20 of them usually buys more time with your buyers for less.

How do I host a side event at Stockholm Fintech Week?

Pick one sharp question, book a venue for 15 to 40 people early, co-host with a brand your guests trust, and send personal invitations at least four weeks ahead. 17 March 2027 is the official side-event day.
