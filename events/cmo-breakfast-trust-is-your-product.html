CMO Breakfast: Trust Is Your Product and Also Your Constraint

Marketing with stakes: a breakfast for CMOs on speed, trust and staying sane, hosted at Heijbel Growth.

When
Thursday 11 June 2026
Where
Heijbel Growth, Bondegatan 21, 116 33 Stockholm
Hosts
Alexandra Grisanti, Emilia Nygren, Lorenzo Alessandri, Solveig Rundquist
Presented by
Operator Network, with Heijbel Growth
Format
Invite only. Every seat is approved by the host.

The conversation

The third CMO Breakfast with Heijbel Growth took on marketing where the stakes are personal: health tech, where companies market sensitive decisions such as mental health and fertility, and sell into systems that move slowly, like hospitals, regions and insurers.

The questions on the table

  • When speed and brand consistency collide, what is non-negotiable and what can give a little? Has that line moved in health and wellness compared with other sectors?
  • How do you set the urgency of a message when the topic itself needs patience and care?
  • When you sell into hospitals, regions and insurers, is looking too fast or too loud a commercial risk?
  • What is the one thing marketing owns in your company that no one else could do, and does your leadership know it?

The series

CMO Breakfasts are a series Alexandra Studio runs with Heijbel Growth: Inspiration, Plot-Twist & Authenticity in Marketing Strategy on 17 February, Enduring Speed while Building Trust on 24 March, and this one on 11 June.
