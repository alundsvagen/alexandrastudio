What it is

Conference days are crowded and short on time. The conversations that lead somewhere usually start once the badges come off. The Post Tech Arena VIP Dinner gives a small group a table, a good meal and time to talk after the main event.

Why a side event

A booth buys ten-minute conversations with people who are also there to sell. A side event buys a few hours with the people you actually want to meet. We wrote about the trade-off in Agency, hire or booth?

How to join

Every seat is approved by the host, Alexandra Grisanti. Request to join on Luma at least two weeks before the event; registration closes on Thursday 21 January 2027. Approved guests receive the exact address in Stockholm and the details for the evening.
