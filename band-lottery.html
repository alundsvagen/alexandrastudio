How it works

Fate picks your bandmates — fate, and us, with a spreadsheet. We match you by instrument and experience level and drop you into a WhatsApp group with your new band. From there the band is yours: you choose your cover, you write your original, you decide when and where to rehearse. We handle the matchmaking and host the final show. You handle the magic.

The five weeks

Now Register. Tell us your instrument and experience level. That's the whole audition. All instruments welcome — guitar, drums, sax, accordion, nyckelharpa, theremin, kazoo, and yes, spoons.
Aug 19 Registration closes. The sorting happens and the bands are locked. Last day for a full refund.
Aug 22 Band reveal & fika. We meet in person at a lokal on Ringvägen. You meet your band face to face, come up with a band name together, and we have fika.
5 weeks Rehearse your way. Your band picks the songs, the space, and the schedule. It's your band — we're just the matchmakers.
Sep 27 The final show. Ten minutes on stage: one cover, one original. Friends and family invited.

The honest part

Is this a scam?

No. Band Lottery is produced by Alexandra Studio, a registered Swedish business based in Stockholm that produces events professionally. You can see who we are, what we've built, and find us on LinkedIn.

What if I change my mind?

Full refund, no questions asked, straight back to you via Swish — any time until August 19, when the sorting is final and the bands are locked.

What does 300 kr cover?

The band matching, the kickoff fika, the final show venue and production, and professional photography of the night.
