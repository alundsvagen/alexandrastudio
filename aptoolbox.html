FAQ

What does Alexandra Studio do?

B2B growth marketing for European fintechs, run from Stockholm. Instead of paid advertising, we co-host in-person events with companies who already sell to your buyer, and turn each room into content that reaches everyone who could not attend. The output is relationships and a contact list you own.

Who do you work with?

B2B fintechs of roughly 15 to 25 people, mostly in embedded finance infrastructure, SME lending, and payments and invoicing technology. One client per vertical at a time, so you never share a room or a partner with a competitor.

Where should I start?

If you want the argument, read Problems in B2B Marketing. If you want evidence, read the Return on Investment case studies. If you already know what you need, book a call — fifteen minutes, no deck.

How do I get in touch?

Book a discovery call directly, or find Alexandra Grisanti on LinkedIn. She also writes Stockholm Growth & Fintech for more than 1,200 Nordic operators, and runs the Operator Network, an application-only C-suite community in Stockholm.
